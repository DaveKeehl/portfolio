It's been a long road, but I'm so excited to finally be able to share the latest version of my portfolio with the world! After almost two years of development and iteration, I'm proud to be able to launch version 4 of my website.

Each year, I take the time to update my site to showcase my current skills and new projects I've been working on. It's also a great opportunity for me to experiment with ideas and technologies that I wouldn't normally get to explore in a professional capacity.

I don't want to give too much away, but I'm hope you'll enjoy the in-depth case study I've prepared to share the story behind the design and development process. All I can say is, I haven't had this much fun putting together a website in a long time!

In the future, I plan to maintain the current version of my website, rather than start from scratch each year.
